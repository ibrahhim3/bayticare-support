Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use BaytiCare, we collect your phone number (for login) and your physical location/address (to send service professionals to your home).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your location data is used strictly for fulfilling home service requests. Your phone number is used for account security and communication regarding your bookings.

3. Account & Data Deletion

You can completely delete your account and wipe all associated personal data at any time by opening the BaytiCare app, navigating to the Profile tab, and tapping "Delete Account".
